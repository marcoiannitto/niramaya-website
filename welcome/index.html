How it works

The science, simplified.

Think of your body as having three fuel tanks.

⚡
Sugar
Your quick energy. Small tank, runs out fast.
💪
Muscle
The engine that keeps you strong and burns energy all day.
🔋
Fat
Your big backup tank, holding weeks of energy.

Here is the problem with normal diets. When you just eat less, your body gets scared. It thinks food is running out. So it protects the big fat tank for "later" and burns your muscle instead.

The science: when sugar runs low, your body needs to make more. The easiest way is to break down muscle. Fat is harder to use, so your body holds onto it.

That is why most diets fail. The scale drops, so you think it is working. But a lot of what you are losing is muscle, not just fat. And less muscle means a slower engine, so the weight creeps back, usually as fat. You end up softer and heavier than when you started.

AEVUM flips this. It convinces your body that food is not running out, so it stops protecting the fat and starts burning it. We do this three ways.

1

We take away the fear.

You eat less, but in a way that keeps your body calm. It feels fed, not starving. So it stops guarding the fat.

2

We protect your muscle.

The food and supplement keep your muscle supplied, so your body has no reason to burn it. Your engine stays intact.

The science: give the body enough protein and it leaves the muscle alone. It turns to fat instead.
3

We keep the engine running smooth.

Burning fat uses up vitamins, minerals, and salts. We keep those topped up, so you stay full of energy instead of feeling flat and tired.

The result

Your body burns its own fat, keeps its muscle, and you feel good while it happens.

7,700 cal

Energy stored in 1 kg of fat. Nearly four days of fuel. You are carrying more than you think.

Beyond the mirror

Fat loss is not cosmetic. It's structural.

  • Muscle is where your body stores and controls sugar. Preserve your muscle mass and your body handles sugar better in the long term.
  • The fat that ages you is not the fat you can pinch. It is the deep fat wrapped around your organs.
  • Deep visceral fat is one of the most important markers of long-term health, and it is the first one to go during this program.

This is not about fitting into something. It's about how your body functions for the next 20 years.

The rules

Clear, simple, non-negotiable.

Tap each card to expand.

💧Supplement (serum)▼
  • First 10 days only (Loading + Activation).
  • Twice daily: morning, and evening before dinner.
  • Shake the vial. Hold under your tongue for 1 minute. Swallow.
🥗Food during Activation▼
Eat
  • Lean protein (chicken breast, prawns, white fish, tuna, tofu, tempeh)
  • Vegetables (~150g, low carb)
  • Fruit with sugar below 12g (~100g)
Avoid
  • Added sugar, carbs, all fats and oils (no butter, no oil, no pork belly, no salmon)
  • Starchy vegetables, sauces with sugar
  • Dairy: not allowed during Activation
  • Carbs: below 30g

The full list is in the app. If you're unsure about any food, ask the AI or your coach.

🍴Food during Loading (first 2 days)▼
  • Eat freely. No restrictions. Aim for 50 to 100% above your normal intake.
📈Food during Stabilization▼
  • Fats reintroduced gradually (3 to 4 day ramp-up).
  • No calorie limit (stay below your BMR). High protein. Carbs below 40g (from vegetables and berries).
  • Your target: keep your weight stable within 500 to 700g of your Reset result.
  • Why: your body needs time to accept the new weight as its baseline. A sudden surplus tells the body the famine is over, and it stores fat again.
⏰Meal timing▼
  • Intermittent fasting during Activation: 16:8, ideally 18:6.
  • Optimal eating window: 10am to 6pm.
💧Water▼
  • Minimum 2.5 litres daily, all 31 days. Non-negotiable.
  • The metabolic process flushes water from your body. Not drinking enough causes dehydration and constipation.
☕Drinks▼
  • Coffee and tea: yes (no milk, no sugar).
  • Zero-calorie drinks: yes. Sweeteners: yes.
🚫Alcohol▼
  • During Activation: strictly off limits.
  • During Stabilization: low-sugar spirits only (gin, whiskey), no mixers. Limited to two drinks across the entire Stabilization phase.
🏃Exercise▼
  • During Activation: keep to 60 to 80% of your normal effort. No running or high-intensity work.
  • Your heart rate may feel elevated during exercise. That's normal (your body is working harder to convert fat to fuel). If something feels off, consult your doctor.
🍫Snacking (Activation)▼
  • Only within your eating window.
  • Allowed: konjac jelly, high-percentage dark chocolate.
  • Best kept to the first few days. Spacing your meals without snacking gives better results.
Watch out

The small things that trip people up.

1

Hidden sugars in sauces. Extremely common in Asian cooking. Check before you eat.

2

Starchy vegetables. Potato, sweet potato, corn. They look harmless. They're not during Activation.

3

Not reading ingredients. One wrong item can interrupt your metabolic state for a full day.

4

Not drinking enough water. 2.5 litres is the floor, not the ceiling.

5

Overeating. Even allowed foods, in excess, slow your progress. Use the app to track.

What to expect

Your body will tell you it's working.

Energy

A dip is possible on day 1 to 2 of Activation. After that, most people feel a significant energy boost from the metabolic shift.

Hunger

Drops noticeably after the first couple of days. Mental clarity and steady energy replace the usual hunger patterns.

Mood

Generally very positive. Low mood is rare and short-lived (day 1 to 2 at most).

Sleep

Expect to sleep very well.

Constipation

Possible, due to low food volume. Fix: drink 2.5L or more. If it persists beyond a few days, take magnesium citrate.

If you slip up

It's not the end of the world. But here's what happens.

  • You'll likely see the effect on the scale the next morning.
  • You may feel an energy dip (your metabolic state was interrupted).
  • The plan is designed to recover. One slip does not erase your progress but can slow it down.
  • But staying strict through the Reset is the goal. The program is kept short on purpose: 13 days of focus is achievable.

Practical tips

A few things that make the 31 days easier.

  • Social situations. Time the program so Activation avoids travel if possible. If you're eating out, a salad with grilled protein works. Avoid sauces and oil.
  • Women. Avoid overlapping Activation with your menstrual cycle. Water retention and hormonal changes add unnecessary difficulty to an already focused phase.
  • Medications. There is no data on interactions. If you're taking anything, talk to your doctor before starting.
